--- layout: home title: MUNGO subtitle: Memorial University of Newfoundland and Labrador's Geophysics Outreach --- ## Bringing Geophysics into Newfoundland and Labrador Classrooms MUNGO (Memorial University of Newfoundland and Labrador's Geophysics Outreach) is an outreach initiative focused on helping students discover Earth Science and geoscience careers through hands-on learning and classroom-ready resources. We focus our work alongside educators, industry professionals, and geophysics faculty to make geoscience more engaging, local, and curriculum-linked for K-12 students across Newfoundland and Labrador.
