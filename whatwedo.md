### What We Do

MUNGO bring geophysics out of the university and into classrooms and communities across Newfoundland and Labrador. Our activities include:

- Classroom visits with hands-on activities
- Teacher support & curriculum resources
- Public geophysics outreach events
- University lab demonstrations for teachers and students
- Student engagement in geoscience careers in our province
